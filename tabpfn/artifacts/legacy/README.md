# Results imported from the earlier TabPFN study

These files come unchanged from the earlier single-model study
(`compas-data/COMPAS-data/studies/tabpfn/artifacts/`, commit `8bfc64a`). They are kept as
reference and are **not** regenerated by anything in this repo.

## How that study differs from this one

| | Earlier study | This repo |
|---|---|---|
| Model | `TabPFNClassifier(random_state=42)`, library defaults | `pfn_model.build_model()`: `n_estimators=8`, `fit_with_cache` |
| Holdout AUC | 0.7355 | 0.7358 |
| Test set | stratified 70/30, seed 42 (1,852 rows) | the same split (`data.split_index`) |
| Designs | holdout, all features only | holdout, X1/X2/X3, two time windows × 7 feature sets |
| Threshold | **0.21**, the cost-optimal threshold *read off the test set* | **0.5** and **0.252** (break-even), fixed in advance |
| Costs | $40k per missed re-offence, $13.5k per unneeded detention | same |

So the two models are close but not identical. The results below are the ones that do not
depend on the threshold, and they are safe to quote with this caveat.

### Decision stability, recomputed at this repo's thresholds

The two score matrices are stored, so the decision counts can be redone at the new thresholds
without refitting. "Unstable" follows the earlier study's definitions:
- bootstrap: a defendant flagged in more than 5% and fewer than 95% of the refits;
- row order: a defendant whose decision is not the same in all 20 shuffles.

At 0.21 the numbers match the ones the earlier study reported (19.4% and 2 decisions).

| Threshold | Bootstrap refits: unstable decisions | Row order: decisions that change |
|---|---|---|
| 0.21 (earlier study) | 19.4% | 2 of 1,852 |
| **0.252** (break-even) | **24.4%** | **23 of 1,852** |
| 0.5 | 20.5% | 13 of 1,852 |

## What was not imported, and why

Everything that depends on the 0.21 threshold was left behind: the fairness tests and gaps,
TOST, FPDP, mitigation, cost curves, permutation importance and XPER on cost, and the
significance tests that compare decisions. These are redone at 0.5 and 0.252 in
`tabpfn/artifacts/analysis/`. The comparisons with the other five models of the earlier
roster are also left out, since those models are not part of this folder.
